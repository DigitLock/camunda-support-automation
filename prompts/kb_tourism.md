# Tourism support knowledge base (kb_tourism)

Grounding material for `ticket.answer` (design D5-11). Each section has a stable id that
answers must cite in `usedKbIds`. Deliberately free of amounts, prices, limits and
deadlines: those are booking-specific and live in the customer's booking confirmation,
fare rules or hotel voucher — answers point there instead of quoting a figure.

## KB-01 Baggage allowance

The baggage allowance depends on the airline, the fare and the route. The exact
allowance for a booking is printed on the e-ticket and in the booking confirmation under
"Baggage". Charter flights usually have a smaller allowance than scheduled flights, and
infants without a seat have their own rule. Extra baggage can be bought before departure
through the booking and is cheaper than paying at the airport. Sports equipment and
musical instruments are handled as special baggage and must be registered in advance.

## KB-02 Check-in and boarding

Online check-in opens at the time stated by the airline for the specific flight; the
booking confirmation links to it. Airport check-in closes before departure at the time
printed on the e-ticket. Boarding passes can be printed or shown on a phone. For charter
flights online check-in is often unavailable, and passengers check in at the airport
counter. Customers with reduced mobility or travelling with infants should tell us in
advance so assistance can be arranged.

## KB-03 Hotel check-in and check-out

Hotel check-in and check-out times are set by the hotel and stated on the hotel voucher.
Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and are requested from the
hotel directly or through us before arrival. On arrival the guest shows the voucher and a
passport. The room category is the one named on the voucher; upgrades on site are
between the guest and the hotel.

## KB-04 Passports, visas and entry documents

The customer is responsible for valid travel documents. Passport validity requirements
and visa rules depend on the destination and the traveller's citizenship and can change;
we point to the official source (the destination's consulate or the airline's document
check) rather than restating rules. Children need their own documents, and a child
travelling with one parent may need consent from the other. We do not issue visas but can
provide a booking confirmation for a visa application.

## KB-05 Payment and instalments

A booking is confirmed once the deposit or full amount stated in the booking is received.
Accepted payment methods are those shown at checkout: bank card and bank transfer. The
remaining balance, if any, is due by the date stated in the booking confirmation; an
unpaid balance can lead to cancellation under the fare or tour conditions. Card payments
are charged in the booking currency; the customer's bank may apply its own conversion.

## KB-06 Invoices and payment receipts

An invoice or receipt for a paid booking is available on request and is issued to the
name and details on the booking. A receipt for the deposit and a final invoice after full
payment are both possible. Invoices for a company need the company details — company name, tax identification
number and legal address — before the invoice is issued; we cannot change the payer name
afterwards.

## KB-07 Changing a booking

Changes to dates, names, room or seat category and upgrades are requested through the
booking or by contacting support with the booking reference. Whether a change is
possible, and what it costs, follows the fare rules and hotel conditions of that booking.
Name corrections of a few letters are usually simpler than a full name change. Changes
close to departure are more restricted. A change request is confirmed in writing before
it is applied.

## KB-08 Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation conditions and any refund depend on the fare rules and tour conditions of
the booking; they are stated in the booking confirmation. Refunds go back to the original
payment method and take the time the payment provider needs. If the airline or hotel
cancels, the customer is entitled to a rebooking or a refund under the applicable rules.
A refund status question is answered with the booking reference.

## KB-09 Travel insurance

Travel insurance is optional unless the destination requires it. Insurance bought with
the booking is listed on the booking confirmation together with the insurer's contact
details and the policy number. Claims are made with the insurer, not with us; we provide
booking documents for the claim on request. Insurance covering cancellation must be bought
at the time of booking.

## KB-10 Transfers and excursions

Airport transfers and excursions included in a package are listed on the voucher with the
meeting point and the provider's contact. Transfer times follow the flight schedule; if a
flight changes, the transfer is updated by us. Optional excursions are booked with the
local provider or through us before travel.

## KB-11 Contacting an agent

Questions outside this knowledge base, complaints, and anything that depends on the
specific booking (amounts, deadlines, availability) are passed to a support agent, who
answers with the booking reference at hand. The customer does not need to repeat the
request; the ticket already carries it.
